To register for My NHS Pension, you'll need to enter your:

  • National Insurance number
  • NHS Pensions membership number
  • 2 unique registration codes

You'll then need to:

  • create a username and password
  • enter your email address
  • verify your email

Use a personal email address

You should use a personal email address rather than a work one. This is because you may need to access My NHS Pension after you've left NHS employment.

Information:

We'll send a verification email to this address. If you do not verify your email address, your account may be locked.

Help finding my membership number

This is an 8 digit number, which may start with 'SD'. You can find it on payslips, annual benefit statements, Total Reward Statements, or any letters from us.

Help finding my registration codes

These are your unique registration codes. There will be 2 of them. Find them on any emails we've sent you with information about registering for My NHS Pension, or by contacting us.

For more information on registering you can go to My NHS Pension (opens in new tab).
