You cannot register for My NHS Pension if you:

  • are a child dependant of a member who has died
  • have a pension credit record from divorce or dissolution of a civil partnership
  • left the Scheme with less than 2 years or membership
  • do not have any membership left in the Scheme due to claiming a refund or transferring to another pension scheme
  • receive an injury benefit pension
  • are acting on a member's behalf, even if you have power of attorney

I should be able to register

If you've read this guidance and think you should still be able to register you can use our online form to Contact NHS Pensions.
