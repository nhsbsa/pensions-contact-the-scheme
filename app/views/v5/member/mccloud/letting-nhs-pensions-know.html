Once you have decided which pension benefits you want to receive for the remedy period you need to let NHS Pensions know.

Information:

NHS Pensions

We'll update your pension benefits within 60 days of receiving your Decision Form back.

If your decision means your benefits will change, we'll send you a statement confirming your updated benefits.
