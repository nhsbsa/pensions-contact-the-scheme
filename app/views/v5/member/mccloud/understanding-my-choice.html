In your RSS, we'll ask you to choose the benefits you want for your membership between 1 April 2015 and 31 March 2022. This is called the remedy period.

You can decide to take your remedy period pension benefits from either the:

  • 1995 or 2008 Scheme, depending on which one you were a member of before 1 April 2022
  • 2015 Scheme

Your RSS will show your personalised calculations to help you choose the best option for you.
