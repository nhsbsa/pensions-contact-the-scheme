In your RSS, we'll ask you to choose the benefits you want for your membership between 1 April 2015 and 31 March 2022. This is called the remedy period.

You can decide to take your remedy period pension benefits from either the:

  • 1995 or 2008 Scheme, depending on which one you were a member of before 1 April 2022
  • 2015 Scheme

Your RSS will show your personalised calculations to help you choose the best option for you.

Find out more about making your remedy choice (opens in new tab).

Information:

On the letter you should see a code that starts with 'RSS'. This code corresponds with further guidance videos on our website. Select the relevant link to your letter and you can watch the video for more information.

You can learn more about your RSS letter on the understanding your remedy choice letter (opens in new tab) page.
